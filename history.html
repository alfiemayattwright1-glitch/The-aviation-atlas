Aviation History Archive

A chronological journey through the engineering breakthroughs that redefined global human mobility.

1903

The Wright Flyer at Kitty Hawk

Orville and Wilbur Wright accomplish the first successful controlled, sustained, powered heavier-than-air flight in North Carolina.

1927

Solo Transatlantic Crossing

Charles Lindbergh flies the Spirit of St. Louis non-stop from New York to Paris in 33.5 hours, captivating global public imagination.

1952

Inauguration of Commercial Jet Travel

The de Havilland Comet enters revenue passenger service with BOAC, introducing smooth, high-altitude commercial jet travel.

1969

Maiden Flights: 747 & Concorde

A landmark year featuring both the first flight of the Boeing 747 "Queen of the Skies" widebody airliner and the supersonic Concorde.

1988

Digital Fly-by-Wire Era

The Airbus A320 enters service pioneering full digital fly-by-wire flight control systems in commercial airliners.
